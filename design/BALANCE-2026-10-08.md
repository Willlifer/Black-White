# Balance pass, 2026-10-08 (D473-D478)

Measured with `tools/campaign_sim.gd` and its new ledger (D473), on the whole
rules set after Keystones v3, kits v2/v3 and systems pass 5. The AI plays both
sides, so read trends, not single cells: a 48-run sample moves ±7-10 points
per card, and two 48-run samples at different seeds disagreed by up to 30
points on the small cards (the Twins, Storm: 16-36 fights each).

## How it was run

- **Card policy (D353), mixed rooms, 48 runs** (`ROOMS=mixed POLICY=mixed`,
  seeds 9000-9047, 12 shards of 4): the AI's default picks (`auto_choice`: the
  first card, so always the element's first keystone and never a duo perk).
- **Random picks, 96 runs** (`PICKS=random`, seeds 9000-9047 and 20000-20047):
  every pick is a random unowned card, so all 14 keystones and the duo perks
  get measured. Pick win rates below come from this set.
- **Hard rooms, 24 runs** (`ROOMS=hard`): Hard taken at every Standard / Hard
  offer.
- The card policy plays both 3v3 and 6v6: fights 5, 8 and 10 are 6v6 cards,
  7 and 9 alternate a 3v3 room and a 6v6 card (the 3v3 when hurt).
- Shards merge with `tools/ledger_merge.gd` (`DIR=<dir of LEDGER_OUT files>`).
- Targeted sims for the knobs: `BOSS=twins|obelisks STOP_AT=4`, `STOP_AT=3
  THREE=…`, and `castle_sim CAMPAIGN=<snapshots> MODE=storm CFM=storm:8:x`.

## Targets

Normal fights 75-85%; Hard rooms and bosses 55-70%; the Giant beatable; fights
not dragging (the openers and the Twins ran long).

Per card the heal share is 2-5% everywhere but the Twins. **Healing is not
outsized**: no source moves a fight. No heal numbers were changed. The one
fight that healing drags is the Twins, and that was their HP (D477), not the
heal: `HEAL_PCT` 2.0 → 1.5 left their rounds at 10.3.

**Stalls (L-46):** 0 of 2,112 fights hit the 1,500-turn guard before the pass
(5 ran 20+ rounds, all Twins), and `test_campaign`'s seed no longer stalls. The
stall is rare, but nothing stopped it, so **Fatigue (D474)**: from round 15
every heal is halved, from round 20 heals do nothing. Normal fights end by
round 6-9 and never meet it. Chosen over "no light heal for a unit that didn't
move" because that one would also tax a player holding a light tile on
purpose in a normal fight (1.3% of HP lost is unmoved healing), and over a
damage ramp because it only removes the thing that causes the standoff.

## Concerns for the author (design level, not changed)

1. **La Niña's pull.** Cutting its damage 40% barely moved it (+14.7 → +11.8).
   Pulling every foe 1 toward the holder at every turn start, board-wide, is
   the strength: it feeds the squad's melee and painted ground for free. Options:
   pull only foes within 4, or every other turn, or only when the holder
   didn't attack last turn.
2. **Bows read strong in the sim (+7 to +8 in both samples, ~4σ)** even
   after Pressured. The sim's AI kites well with a bow (L-7), so this may be
   the AI, not the class; worth a look in play.
3. **Superconductor (+7) and Being of Rain (+5 to +7)** are consistently
   above average but inside two standard errors. Watch, don't cut yet.
4. **Duo perks are almost never offered** (4% of fights with random picks):
   a duo needs an ordinary perk in both elements before it's even drawn, and
   then only as the second card on 35% of picks. If they're meant to be
   build-defining, they need a gentler gate.
5. **Leviathan barely happens**: 5 submerges in 190 fights held. "End your own
   walk on water 3" is rare without a water painter beside you; the AI can't
   set it up. In play a player can, but the keystone is invisible to the sim.
6. **Overload and Kindle are never used** by the AI (Overload only fires when
   its blasts hurt foes more than its side; Kindle when nothing else is in
   hand). Their value is unmeasured. The dagger's spellblade kit (D432) is
   the place to look.
7. **Light heals the other side.** Half of all light-tile healing is a unit
   healing on its *enemy's* light (1.4% of player HP lost). That's the rule
   (light heals the occupant; Glare is the perk that stops it), but it's
   probably not what players expect.
8. **The openers are the longest normal fights** (7-7.7 rounds vs 5.5-6.5 for
   the rest). In range, but if fights 1-2 feel slow, the knob is enemy HP at
   fights 1-2 (no HP column on ENEMY_CURVE yet), not the stat scale.
9. **Fatigue has no on-screen announcement yet** (the battle emits a `fatigue`
   event at rounds 15 and 20; no feed line or glossary row: those are the
   text lane's files).
