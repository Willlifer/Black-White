# Sessions 2026-10-04 to 10-05: from kickoff to a polished run

This summarises the first two days of Black | White, for orientation. The
details live in DECISIONS (D1–D163+) and the design docs.

## Day 1 (10-04): foundation to a dressed, animated, playable loop

**Kickoff and interview.**
- The author's brief (`design/KICKOFF.md`) was reviewed alongside the V8 Temporal Sea project.
- The interview set the core intent: no story, no main character, B/W low poly, with hair (= element) as the only hue. The finish line is "a game I can open that I can send units through a lil adventure".
- V8 was ported for its combat mechanics only (the charge grid, weapon skills, the turn and hex framework).
- Life was dropped and staff added. WIL/WIS/INT were merged into willpower. Stats run 1–6 at the start, capped at 1000.
- Work runs in gated phases (`PLAN.md`), with every call logged in DECISIONS.

**Phases 0–2.**
- A fresh `game/` project. A pure rules core, where every number carries a formula breakdown. Data lives in CSV.
- The self-test runner and the UI/flow probes.
- Five maps, a greybox combat screen, the pre-battle, roster and downtime screens, and the full run structure.
- Author notes along the way: 100 XP per level; HP changed to 100 + 2×CON.

**Phase 3, art.**
- The Blender rig: stick figure, xkcd/Mint-Chan proportions, inverted-hull ink outlines with a white contour on black limbs.
- 10 hair archetypes, 14 garments × 3 greys, 22 armour pieces × element variants, 22 weapons plus fists.
- The roster was dressed. The author approved the rig and the style. The hair was restyled away from the "bucket hat / coconut" look and lost its sheen.

**Phase 4, animation.**
- A style bar (ZZZ-like flow, in stick figure), then the full matrix: walks, strikes per class, casts, channels, and hit variants (shrug, stagger-recover, flinch, knockback, enraged).
- Idles were toned down after "the most bouncy thing I have ever seen". Weapon-handling idles were added.

**V8 ports.** The camera rig, the movement/attack radius visuals, Esc to undo a move, and a HUD locked to a right-side slot.

## Day 2 (10-05): presentation, audio, then a big design pass

**Phases 5–6.**
- Title screen, marble hall downtime, the hall prep stop before fight 1 (with a 5-piece starting kit), and animated element tiles.
- Procedural SFX and adaptive music from the author's loops: a battle set at 108 BPM, a boss set, and a brighter octave layer.
- Loss continues the run.

**Balance review and rules.**
- The downtime numbers were rebalanced.
- Thunder tiles conduct: chain lightning arcs 50% to the nearest teammate. Thunder hits on uncharged ground get Spark (+10%); attacks on glazed hexes get ice Shatter (+15%).
- A skills pass gave every skill a hook (Riposte is free with a 3-turn cooldown; the Striketwice reactions).

**The pick system.**
- Element perks: 5 per element, picked at affinity ranks 1 and 2, all five at rank 3.
- Weapon skill picks per expertise letter: learn a skill, or Improve one (once per skill).
- A skill registry with one file per skill, and 40 new skills (5 per weapon class).
- Status rework: Staggered = no skills (then Steadied); Blinded = no crit, reach 2; Pinned = −2 move. The author dislikes "−hit" effects.
- Facing matters: rear attacks gain hit, frontal hits glance more. Wind on wind makes a Gale 2.
- The content was reviewed on the author's phone through the "Black | White Picks" Claude Doc.

**Presentation.**
- Skill callouts (slimmed down after feedback), the crit flash (freeze, white, collapse to a line), and an odds strip during cutscenes.
- Cutscene tiers (minimal / short / full), hold-Space to skip, and F to cycle modes.
- A settings menu and pause menu, a 75-term glossary on hover, and battle and run summaries.

**Maps.**
- Static (renewing) and seeded (start charged, changeable) element tiles.
- Four new maps: Lake, Catacombs, Forge, Chapel.
- Fight 4 is the Obelisks objective fight: break either stone. They have 350 HP and pulse 10 each, one pushing, one pulling.
- The map order is shuffled per run, except fight 4 and the Giant.

**Downtime redesigned (author spec).** One choice per unit, with no information shown:
- **Specialize:** half ranks, bonus picks or found gear.
- **Branch out:** pick one of two cards, each a new element plus a new weapon class.
- **Wander:** nine 20% rolls (next-fight resists and buffs, items, a recruit), +100 XP if all fail, and a rare +5000 XP "Disobedient" rogue.
- Results show one unit at a time, in first person.

**Difficulty.**
- Enemies were scaled by stage (fights 1–2 easier; the lag shrinks to level by fights 9–10).
- HP became 100 + 2×CON + 10×level. Enemy armour ramps 0/1/2/full.
- A whole-run AI campaign sim backs the tuning.

**Roster.**
- The author's 20 names (Alexandra replaced "Salmon"). Aureli is light-locked and Rem ice-locked.
- Identity is fixed; weapon, element and clothes are seeded-random per run, with a Randomize button. Stats follow the weapon.
- Personality lines were removed.

**Hair framework.** Colour follows affinity rank. Rank 1 has 30% contrast streaks, white on dark elements and black on light ones; units with several elements get an ombré. An early mostly-black version looked "samey" and was reverted.

**Polish pass.**
- Real portraits: live camera feeds for the big cards, cached snapshots for the icons.
- A blast preview with chain arcs and friendly-fire warnings, recap lines, tile hover cards, and a slow-motion beat on blasts.
- Real bow animation with arrows; Arcing Shot and Rain of Arrows as volleys rather than circles.
- Element-shaped cast releases (Surge and Ley Line especially), and spectacles for the big moves.
- Hit feel: shake, hit-stop, scaled numbers.

**Process.**
- Up to four agents ran in parallel, each with owned files and a reserved DECISIONS range. One context compaction happened mid-session.
- Docs were reorganised after reviewing V8's CLAUDE/HANDOFF/LEDGER/MEMORY: the slim `CLAUDE.md` index, `HANDOFF.md`, `LEDGER.md`, and this history folder.
- Git began: a full internal repo, plus a barebones export to GitHub (`Willlifer/Black-White`).
